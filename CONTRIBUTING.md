# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing! A few things to know before
you open a pull request.

## License and contributor agreement

This project is maintained by The Artificial Creative B.V. (KvK 61953679)
and is source-available under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md): free to use, modify,
and share for noncommercial purposes, with commercial licensing handled
separately by the maintainer.

To keep that dual-licensing model workable, all contributions require
agreement to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). In short: you
keep the copyright to your contribution, and you grant the maintainer a
license to distribute and relicense it — including under commercial
terms. Without this, the project could not accept outside contributions
and still offer commercial licenses covering the whole codebase.

**How to agree:** include the following line in the description of your
first pull request:

> I have read and agree to the Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md).

Additionally, sign off each commit (`git commit -s`) to certify, per the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org),
that you have the right to submit the work:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Pull requests without CLA agreement and commit sign-offs cannot be
merged.

## Practical notes

- **Repo conventions** (GitOps, migrations, terminology, link rules)
  live in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
- **Architecture orientation**: start with
  [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) and [docs/README.md](docs/README.md).
- Keep pull requests focused; separate refactoring from behavior
  changes where practical.
- If you are including any third-party code, identify it and its
  license in the pull request description (see CLA §4.4).
