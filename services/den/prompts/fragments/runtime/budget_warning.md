Budget advisory: runtime budgets, including wall-clock budget, are per-turn execution limits. They are safety rails for the current slice of work, not a signal that a focused Job or task list is complete. If focused work remains and you are near a budget, avoid trying to force the whole Job through this turn: checkpoint or record progress, decompose remaining work into smaller task/subtask slices when task tools are available, and continue in a fresh turn/run when the runtime allows it.
